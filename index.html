The Little Lamp That Never Gave Up Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a little lamp named Lumi. Every night, Lumi tried to shine as brightly as the big lamps in the village square, but her light was small. “I’m too weak to make a difference,” Lumi sighed. One stormy night, a little boy got lost in the forest. The big lamps could not help him because the storm had knocked them out.
